Scope: "/" landing + sign-in (Persuade). Visitor: someone with a few machines at home or in a small office and no IT person. Action: Try the demo (one click, anonymous) or Sign in with Google, visible the whole time. Proof: the real product loop played on a simulated sample home, labelled as such. Constraints: always dark; mascot art traced, never redrawn, flat fills; logo is the outlined wordmark; Funnel Display / Funnel Sans; reduced motion shows still frames. Direction pinned by the user via references (overworldaudio.com world + leoparpeix.com Swiss grid), so no concept roll.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is one dark cutaway house at night that the mascot patrols; devices light up as it checks them, and scrolling pushes the camera into the attic where the product loop plays out for real. Refuses the split hero with feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: Flat layered night navies (#11141f ground, #0d1019 structure, #1a1f30 rooms), mascot ink/off-white, star yellow #fdd83a as the only warm light; soft status green/amber; drifting flat fog bands, stars, moon. Funnel Display heavy/thin contrast like the wordmark; Funnel Sans small precise UI type on hairline rules.
STORY: Visitor sees the house being looked after, then watches it notice, explain, ask (they tap Approve themselves), and earn trust; then reads the sample home ledger, the privacy terms and who it is for, and tries the demo.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky nav (wordmark, three anchors, Sign in + yellow Try the demo). Left column: 2-3 line display headline, one sentence, both actions. Right 62%: the house filling to the ground line, mascot walking floor by floor, markers lighting; fog and hills bleed full-width under the text.
FORM: pinned direction (user references), code-led, seed key: none (pinned).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
