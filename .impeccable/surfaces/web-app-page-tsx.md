Scope: "/" landing + sign-in (Persuade). Visitor: someone with a few machines at home or in a small office and no IT person; judges via the one-click demo. Action: Try the demo (anonymous) or Sign in with Google, always visible in the nav. Proof: the product loop shown in framed product canvases on a simulated sample home, labelled as simulated. Constraints: light monochrome to match the day dashboard; yellow only for the main action and "needs you"; status colours only with a word; no mascot focus, no pixel art, no 3D; outlined wordmark; Funnel Display / Funnel Sans; reduced motion keeps everything still and visible. Direction pinned by the user (cofounder.co reference, 2026-10-18), so no concept roll.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is a walk through the product itself: every chapter is a framed canvas of the real dashboard doing one step of the loop, and the visitor drives it from the list beside it. Refuses the dark hero-with-feature-grid.
OWN-WORLD: Near-white cream ground #fcfbf8, warm near-black ink, a second muted ink for the quiet half of each headline, white cards with hairline #e8e6e0 and soft long shadows, canvases framed on a dot grid. Star yellow only on Try the demo and "needs you". Funnel Display at a light weight for two-tone headlines; Funnel Sans UI type at app scale inside canvases.
STORY: Sees the home being watched (hero canvas, checks ticking in), then four chapters: it notices, explains, asks first (they tap Approve), trust grows; then what leaves the house, then try the demo.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky nav (wordmark, segmented anchor pill, Sign in, yellow Try the demo). Two-tone headline top left, lede and both actions; a wide framed canvas of the dashboard (needs strip, device tiles, wires) starts above the fold with check toasts stacking over it.
FORM: pinned direction (user reference), code-led, seed key: none (pinned).
SIGNATURE: each chapter's accordion drives its canvas: opening "Things that stop" swaps the featured device; opening "Dry run" lights that line on the approval card.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
